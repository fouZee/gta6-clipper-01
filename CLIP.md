# The clip — creative decisions

## The moment (chosen)

**IShowSpeed gets bitten by an alligator in GTA 6, in the Leonida swamps
(Grassrivers), during his 24-hour GTA 6 launch stream.** Speed wades into
the shallows, the alligator lunges and bites him, and his facecam reaction
does the comedy — shock, screaming, then laughing at himself as his
character nearly dies.

## Why this moment will hold a viewer

- The hook is the bite itself, in the first two seconds: an alligator
  attacking the biggest streamer on earth is instantly understandable,
  no context needed.
- Alligators are the iconic GTA 6 wildlife — Leonida is Florida, and the
  gator is the animal every launch-day clip channel picked up, so the
  moment already proved it travels (multiple Shorts and compilations).
- Speed's reaction is the product: people watch Speed for Speed, not for
  the game alone. Opening on his facecam + the bite keeps a viewer past
  the first swipe.

## The cut

- One moment, not a montage. Roughly 20–30 seconds of the bite and the
  reaction, trimmed from the source stream footage.
- Vertical 9:16 (1080x1920), blur-filled crop so Speed's facecam and the
  gameplay both stay in frame; nothing important falls off the sides.
- On-screen caption of Speed's reaction line at the bite.
